# Product

Free includes three research runs, 30 resolves, and up to 40 real managed sends per month with a 20/day cap. Users see reply count and sentiment, while reply body and identity are redacted by the API. Pro is monthly and unlocks full replies, drafts, provider-backed responses, meeting artifacts, and higher safe caps.

Managed mailboxes preserve the product boundary and protect a customer's primary domain. A launch disclosure makes the Free reply lock explicit before sending.
